03 / The approach

Evidence over assumptions.

From an interesting question
to a repeatable result.

[ 01 ]

Understand the surface.

Map the system, its trust boundaries, and the behavior that matters before choosing the tools.

[ 02 ]

Challenge the controls.

Turn a hypothesis into a focused test. Follow the behavior, validate the impact, and keep the evidence.

[ 03 ]

Make the work repeatable.

Document the result, connect the useful tools, and automate the parts that should not need doing twice.
